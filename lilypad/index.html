Teacher notes

Supports 1.OA.B.4, 1.OA.C.5 and 1.OA.C.6 (relating counting to addition and subtraction, and understanding subtraction as an unknown-addend problem). Hop It has students choose the direction before every hop. Which Model? has them read hops and pick the number model; wrong answers explain the mix-up (wrong start or wrong direction). Fact Family has them find all four facts, then names the pattern: plus facts end on the whole, minus facts start on it. Stars reset when the page reloads.
